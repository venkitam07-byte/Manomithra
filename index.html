About Manomithra Institute

Founded in 2008 in Palakkad, Kerala, Manomithra Institute of Medical Sciences Pvt. Ltd. (MIMS) is committed to empowering lives through personalized, ethical, and evidence-based mental healthcare. We combine state-of-the-art medical treatments with empathetic, community-centric approaches.

Our Vision

We envision a highly compassionate world where mental health is prioritized, free from social stigma. With outreach programs like “Breaking the Chains,” we aim to educate school and college students, counsel families, and foster a strong, resilient community.

Our Mission

We believe that effective mental health treatment must be fully personalized. At Manomithra, our goal is to empower every single patient to manage their conditions, overcome chemical dependencies, and reclaim an exceptionally fulfilling and purposeful life.

Collaborative Healing

We practice holistic, multi-disciplinary care. Our treatment designs are formed together by experienced psychiatrists, Ayurvedic doctors, clinical psychologists, speech educators, occupational therapists, and dedicated nursing professionals in complete coordination with the patient's family.
